### Applications & System Design

11. [Verilog Multiplexer Example](https://www.chipverify.com/verilog/verilog-multiplexer)
    - Example Verilog code for 2:1, 4:1, and 8:1 multiplexers, showing practical system design usage.
12. [VHDL Multiplexer Example](https://vhdlwhiz.com/vhdl-mux/)
    - VHDL implementation and explanation of multiplexer design for digital systems.
13. [Multiplexer in Digital System Design - All About Circuits](https://www.allaboutcircuits.com/technical-articles/multiplexers-in-digital-system-design/)
    - Overview of multiplexer applications in digital systems, including system-level integration.
